# 🚀 SQL Data Analytics Project

## 🎯 Project Objective

Developed an **SQL-based Analytics Solution** to analyze large datasets, create meaningful business insights, and generate reports. This project demonstrates advanced **SQL querying**, **data aggregation**, and **data modeling** techniques.

---

## 🏗️ Project Overview

* **Data Sources**: Data extracted from various sources such as CSV files, databases, or APIs (depending on the dataset).
* **Tech Stack**:

  * **SQL Server** or **PostgreSQL** (Database)
  * **SSMS** or **pgAdmin** (Database management)
  * **SQL Scripts** (Data aggregation, cleaning, and reporting)
  * **Excel/Power BI/Tableau** (Visualization & reporting)

---

## ✅ Key Analytics & Insights

### 🔹 Sales Analytics

* **SQL Queries**: Calculated monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends.
* **Key Insights**:

  * Identified top-performing products, sales regions, and time periods.
  * Analyzed seasonality and its impact on sales.

### 🔸 Customer Analytics

* **SQL Queries**: Segmented customers based on demographics, purchasing behavior, and engagement.
* **Key Insights**:

  * Identified high-value customers and buying patterns.
  * Recommended strategies for improving customer retention and loyalty.

### 🏅 Operational Analytics

* **SQL Queries**: Analyzed operational performance, including inventory levels, product turnover, and supplier efficiency.
* **Key Insights**:

  * Identified bottlenecks and areas for operational improvement.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **SQL Server/PostgreSQL**: Database management and query execution.
* **SSMS/pgAdmin**: SQL management tools.
* **Excel/Power BI/Tableau**: Data visualization and reporting.
* **Git & GitHub**: Version control for scripts and project management.
